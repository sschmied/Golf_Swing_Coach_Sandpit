Golf Coach – Enhanced Help

Version enhanced – Pro ghost, tracer, alignment sticks, pressure mat, video export, charts, drills, achievements, metronome, gapping, daily challenge.

New for Usefulness & Enjoyment

  • Alignment sticks: Face-On draws target line (feet line) + ball position line (ideal for shot). DTL draws target line + spine line. Toggle in row2.
  • Ball position checker: Shows ball position % between feet, ideal per shot (driver lead heel, iron center, wedge center-back). Live readout.
  • Shot tracer: Estimates club path from wrist path angle at impact, draws tracer line from impact with fade/draw based on path vs face (estimated from shoulder/hip). Toggle.
  • Pressure mat: Two feet, fill height = weight % lead/trail. At impact ideal 80% lead driver, 50% putt. Visual.
  • Tempo metronome: Click 🥁 Metronome, beeps backswing 3 counts, downswing 1 for driver, 1:1 for putt. Uses Web Audio API.
  • Video export: 🎥 Export Video records skeleton + ghost + tracer + sticks as WebM, save & share with coach.
  • Side-by-Side & Before/After: ↔️ Compare loads second video or saves before, shows improvement in pro score, shoulder turn, tempo.
  • Progress charts: Shoulder turn, tempo, X-factor over time, gapping estimated carry. Motivates practice.
  • Drills library: 20+ drills – alignment, ball position, tempo, rotation, weight shift, head stability.
  • Achievements: Badges – first swing, 10 sessions, 8/10, fast tempo, full turn, daily challenge streak. Fun.
  • Gapping: Estimates carry based on rotation & tempo, driver 220-300y, 7-iron 150y. Calibrate with real.
  • Daily challenge: Hit ideal tempo 3x in a row for selected shot – gamified.
  • Club recommendation: Based on faults – e.g., early extension → check spine, weight shift → pressure mat drill.
